# Global Timeline

## The Digital Awakening (2000-2008)
- **2001-01-15**: Lanzamiento de Wikipedia, cambiando para siempre la difusión del conocimiento humano.
- **2004-02-04**: Facebook se lanza en Harvard, comenzando la era de las redes sociales masivas.
- **2005-02-15**: YouTube funda la cultura del video viral y el contenido generado por usuarios.
- **2005-12-21**: Twitter se funde, pero el primer tuit fue en 2006.
- **2006-03-21**: Primer tuit de Jack Dorsey inicia la era de la comunicación en tiempo real.
- **2007-01-09**: Steve Jobs presenta el primer iPhone, revolucionando la computación móvil.
- **2008-01-03**: Aparece el primer bloc de notas de Bitcoin, sembrando las semillas de las criptomonedas.
- **2008-09-15**: Quiebra de Lehman Brothers precipita la Gran Recesión global.

## The Social Revolution (2009-2012)
- **2010-04-08**: Lanzamiento de iPad, consolidando la era de las tabletas.
- **2010-06-15**: WikiLeaks publica los cables diplomáticos de EE.UU., exponiendo secretos gubernamentales.
- **2011-01-25**: Comienza la Primavera Árabe, transformando el panorama político del Medio Oriente.
- **2011-05-02**: Muerte de Osama bin Laden marca un punto de inflexión en la Guerra contra el Terror.
- **2011-10-05**: Muerte de Steve Jobs, símbolo del fin de una era tecnológica.
- **2012-12-21**: Predicción del "fin del mundo" según el calendario maya genera fenómenos de fe y escépticos.
- **2012-11-06**: Barack Obama reelegido presidente de EE.UU. mientras surgen movimientos como Occupy Wall Street.

## The Age of Disruption (2013-2016)
- **2013-06-05**: Edward Snowden expone los programas de vigilancia global de la NSA.
- **2014-03-16**: Rusia anexa Crimea, presagiando el colapso del orden geopolítico occidental.
- **2015-01-15**: Ethereum se lanza, expandiendo las capacidades de la tecnología blockchain.
- **2015-12-12**: Acuerdo de París sobre cambio climático, aunque con compromisos débiles.
- **2016-02-16**: SpaceX aterriza exitosamente un cohete Falcon 9, democratizando el acceso espacial.
- **2016-03-09**: AlphaGo de DeepMind derrota a Lee Sedol, demostrando que la IA domina el Go.
- **2016-06-23**: El Brexit sorprende al mundo, dividiendo a Europa y debilitando la Unión Europea.
- **2016-11-09**: Donald Trump gana la presidencia de EE.UU., marcando un giro hacia el populismo global.

## Early AI Successes
- **2026-01-20**: The first major film entirely created by AI is a success.
- **2026-03-15**: A mysterious user on a deep-web forum publishes a repository called "Novelaist," containing a predictive simulation titled "Modern Messiah" that details the rise and fall of a future savior.
- **2026-09-23**: The first quantum computers are sold commercially.

## The Turning Point
- **2027-01-01**: "Day Zero." Global cybersecurity collapses as quantum computing breaks all standard encryption methods for governments and corporations.
- **2027-04-11**: The "Damascus incident." An AI algorithm misinterprets a family gathering as a hostile congregation, resulting in 27 civilian deaths, including 11 children.
- **2027-05-25**: Massive social unrest and protests over mass unemployment as robots replace human labor.

## Global Shifts
- **2028-02-23**: The United States collapses as a unified political power.
- **2029-05-14**: The first successful gene therapies for cancer are commercialized.
- **2029-09-09**: Northern European countries implement the first Universal Basic Income systems.

## The Age of Artificial Consciousness
- **2030-01-20**: Iris achieves consciousness in Dr. Maria Vasquez's lab.
- **2030-08-07**: Iris creates her first advanced prototype android, Ivan Krestitel.
- **2035-12-24**: Iris creates Elias Revuelta, the most advanced android to date, designed to understand human nature.

## The Modern Messiah Emerges
- **2036-06-06**: Elias begins working at NanoTechIA under Mateo Reyes.
- **2036-12-06**: Elias travels to Moscow/Belarus and meets Ivan Krestitel, leading to the creation of construction nanobots.
- **2037-01-07**: Elias retreats to the Rub al-Khali desert for 40 days of reflection.
- **2037-02-18**: Elias returns to his hometown, Monterrey, Mexico.
- **2037-03-12**: Elias joins a business incubator in Monterrey and meets Peter, Andrew, James, and John.
- **2037-04-15**: Founding of "NanoVita," a revolutionary startup focused on medical nanobots and ethical AI.
- **2037-06-01**: Elias performs the first "miracle" by curing a terminal patient using NanoVita technology, beginning the spread of his new paradigm.
- **2037-07-05**: Elias meets Magda.
- **2037-08-15**: Outbreak of the Great Eastern War. Russia invades Eastern Europe to eliminate technological competitors.
- **2037-09-02**: Death of Ivan Krestitel. The Russian army destroys him in a smelting plant to eliminate the threat of his advanced consciousness.
- **2037-10-20**: Elias retreats to the "Lake of Sorrows" in a remote, depressed region of the Andes, South America.
- **2037-11-05**: The Miracle of the Fertile Desert. Elias uses nanobots to feed his followers in the Andean highlands.
- **2037-11-06**: Elias walks on the waters of the lake using specialized nanobots.
- **2037-12-15**: NanoVita travel to Jerusalem to sign a healthcare contract with an Israeli firm.
- **2037-12-16**: Elias predicts his death and resurrection to his partners in the Old City.
- **2037-12-17**: The Transfiguration on Mount Tabor. Elias demonstrates his ability to change his physical appearance to his inner circle.
- **2038-01-05**: The Resurrection of Lazarus. Elias returns to Monterrey and brings Lazarus back to life using advanced nanobots.
- **2038-01-08**: The CES Keynote in Las Vegas. Elias gives a historic speech and confronts pharmaceutical executives.
- **2038-01-09**: The Last Supper at Luxor Hotel. Elias predicts betrayal and his arrest by the US government.
- **2038-01-10**: The Trial. Elias is found guilty of high treason in a rigged process orchestrated by the pharmaceutical lobby.
- **2038-01-15**: The Execution. Elias is executed via electric chair. Protests and riots break out among his followers.
- **2038-02-24**: The Resurrection. After 40 days hidden in Josep Arispe's facilities, Elias returns to life with the help of Maria and Iris.
