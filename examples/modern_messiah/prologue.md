# Prologue

I could start explaining the reasons that led me to write this book, but I would be lying because I haven't even written it myself. In the final stages, I didn't even program it. Everything has been achieved thanks to advances in artificial intelligence. Particularly generative ones.

Just like when Johannes Gutenberg invented the printing press, the hand copyists in those ancient monasteries surely thought that this device could not replace them or would never achieve the stroke with the quality they did. There will be people who think that this is not really writing, that I am cheating the art of linking word after word. Actually, I am better at programming or organizing ideas than writing. Perhaps this is the original reason for this initiative.

I do not intend to predict the future, but it is clear that in this 2026 we are experiencing changes that will represent a point of no return for our existence as humans. It depends largely on us whether our future is a utopia or a hell. We are about to evolve as a species in a way that has never occurred in natural history. New intelligent entities are occupying our space. Our non-human children will inherit certain ideas from us. I would like to think that they will inherit our ability to appreciate beauty over our limitless ambition.

I have developed this project to experiment and to have fun. I hope the reader enjoys reading the story as much as I have enjoyed creating it.
